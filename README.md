# CLACK — Daily Tactile Marble Puzzle for Reddit

**CLACK** is a handcrafted daily 3D marble puzzle built natively for Reddit. Each day unlocks a miniature floating diorama island with wooden guide rails, gleaming brass keys, a hanging goal bell, and synchronized rotating switches—all controlled by a single button.

Press once to launch the brass marble. Press again to **CLACK**: every switch on the island snaps together at the same instant. Read the track ahead, collect every brass key, dodge the open trapdoors, and ring the bell at **Par** or better.

---

## How to Play

1. **Launch:** Tap **Launch** (or press `Space` / `Enter`) to release the brass marble from its cradle.
2. **Clack:** Tap anywhere on the island—or hit `Space`—to flip every gate across the board simultaneously between Position A (Circle) and Position B (Diamond).
3. **Collect & Ring:** Route the marble through every golden key to unlock the bell arch, avoid open trapdoors, and reach the bell in as few clacks as possible. Drag gently to inspect the diorama from different angles; press `R` to restart cleanly.

## Fresh Daily Islands & Mechanical Twists

Every day brings a fresh, fair, solver-verified island with a calibrated difficulty rhythm across the week—welcoming early-week boards that ramp smoothly into clever weekend Grand Islands.

Beyond the classic handcrafted biomes (**Cedar Courtyard**, **Mossy Mill**, **Amber Viaduct**, **Frosty Terrace**, and **Highland Dusk**), special Twist Days introduce one spotlight mechanical surprise at a time so every board reads clearly on sight:

- **Broken & Inverted Switches:** Single-use gates that jam after one flip, or contrarian gates that stay opposite the rest of the board.
- **Locked Clack Budgets:** Precision puzzles with a strict clack cap.
- **Teleport Portals & Hinged Drawbridges:** Paired brass portals and high-road / low-road track levers.
- **Counterweight Scales, Timing Lifts & Turntables:** Elevated vertical balances, rhythmic elevators, and 90-degree rotating hubs.
- **Split-Island Swinging Bridges & Canal ("Float") Aqueducts:** Floating twin islands linked by pivoting spans, and sunlit aqueduct channels with buoyant sluice gates.
- **Past Islands Archive:** Revisit and optimize earlier daily islands anytime to sharpen your par record.
